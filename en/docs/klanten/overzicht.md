## An illustrative real-world example

Alongside the real customer cases, Yres uses an anonymized example to illustrate the impact. This is **a hypothetical scenario** (a mid-sized retail company), not a named customer:

**Before Yres** — sales data in one database, marketing campaigns in another, inventory in spreadsheets. Every month IT spent **days** manually merging and cleaning data; managers waited on reports and worked with outdated figures; the company paid for **large VMs** for processing, even during quiet periods.

**After Yres** — within a few days all sources were connected: sales, marketing and inventory were updated automatically and dashboard-ready; managers got near real-time insight; database scaling meant that extra capacity was paid for **only** during heavy processing.

**Result** — reporting time dropped from days back to minutes, faster decisions and a lower monthly Azure bill — "without extra technical staff".

:::note
This example is illustrative (drawn from the Yres training material) and may not be attributed to a specific customer such as Aviko or Paragon.
:::
