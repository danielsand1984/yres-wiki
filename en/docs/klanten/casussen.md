customer-focused cases as well as the illustrative real-world example from the Yres
learning material. Use the overviews below to jump quickly to the right story.

## Illustrative real-world example

Alongside the real cases there is a **hypothetical** real-world example that shows what
Yres means for a typical organization — before and after adoption. This example is
**not** linked to an existing, named customer; it illustrates the pattern that recurs
in the cases above.

- [Real-world example: from days to minutes](../product/use-cases.md) — a
  mid-sized retail company that brings together sales, marketing and inventory data,
  reduces reporting time from days to minutes and lowers the monthly Azure bill
  through smart scaling.

## How these case studies come about

The benefits that recur in the cases — reliable data, faster troubleshooting and
less manual work — rest on the way Yres loads data and keeps history.
Yres lets you **configure sources, tables and load types in wizards** and generates
the ADF pipelines from them; it does not build pipelines that you draw visually by hand.

If you want to understand *why* the results in the cases are achievable, read:

- [Use cases](../product/use-cases.md) — the four pain points that Yres solves.
