## Frequently asked pricing questions

**What is the difference between Essentials and Advanced?**
With Essentials you work in a single environment. Advanced gives you a separate development and production environment, so you can keep developing safely without touching existing dashboards. The changes system and automatic scaling are also exclusive to Advanced and Ultimate.

**Can we scale up later?**
Yes. You can move to a larger plan at any time. We handle the migration without your environment going offline.

**Do our data and pipelines run on your infrastructure?**
By default no: Yres runs within your own Azure tenant and your data never leaves your environment. If you want to hand over management, you can opt for hosting by Yres.
