# Yres explained

> Intended to explain Yres to someone without Azure or data warehouse knowledge — useful for sales and onboarding.

## In plain language

Think of Yres as the **central control room** for all of an organization's data. Instead of information scattered across systems, Yres brings everything together in one secure, easy platform. You don't have to be a techie to connect sources, keep data up to date and make it available for analysis — Yres automates the heavy lifting.

- **Data enabler** — makes data easy to find, connect and use securely.
- **Smart data organizer** — collects information from multiple systems.
- **Reliable transporter** — moves data safely to where it's needed.

## Without vs. with Yres

| Without Yres | With Yres |
|---|---|
| Data in different places and formats | Everything in one secure, central place |
| Reports take days/weeks | Faster, with up-to-date information |
| Errors from outdated/inconsistent data | Automated, fewer human errors |
| Dependent on a few technical experts | Everyone gets the data they need |

## Real-world example

A mid-sized retail company had customer data in multiple systems (sales, marketing, inventory in spreadsheets). IT spent days every month manually combining it; managers worked with outdated reports; and they paid for large VMs, even during quiet periods.

**After Yres:** all sources connected, dashboards automatically up to date, near-real-time insight, and database scaling so that extra capacity was only paid for during peak processing. The result: reporting time from days to minutes and a lower Azure bill — without extra technical staff.

## 10 Unique Selling Points

1. **No-code data management** — manage pipelines without code.
2. **Seamless Azure integration** — ADF, Azure SQL, Blob Storage.
3. **Automatic scaling** — only pay for what you use.
4. **Efficient architecture** — large volumes without expensive VMs.
5. **Fast implementation** — pipelines in hours, not weeks.
6. **Central visibility** — loads, logs and lifecycle in one interface.
7. **Flexible storage** — database, Data Lake or both.
8. **Enterprise-grade security** — Azure RBAC + SSO.
9. **No vendor lock-in** — runs entirely in the customer's own Azure environment.
10. **100% Azure-based** — Yres never has direct access to your sources; the configuration keeps running, even if you stop using Yres.

## Elevator pitch

> "Yres is a cloud platform that connects all your data, keeps it automatically up to date and makes it easy to use — without technical knowledge. It runs entirely in your own Azure environment, saves costs through smart scaling, and gives you full control over your data without vendor lock-in."

:::tip Key selling point
Yres **never has direct access to the customer's data sources**, and all configured data flows keep working within the customer's Azure environment — even without Yres. That is the core of "no vendor lock-in".
:::
