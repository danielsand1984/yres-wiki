# Features

> A data platform you can understand and manage. Yres turns standalone data sources into a standardized data platform in Azure. All the essential functionality for data ingestion, monitoring and management is included by default.

## 1. Connect all your data sources without custom work
Connect systems such as SAP, Salesforce, AFAS and databases directly to your data platform and process data automatically in Azure. Add new data sources without complex scripts or custom work.

## 2. Automate data flows and prevent errors
From raw source data to a structured data warehouse: everything runs automatically and in a controlled way. Smart pipelines and triggers ensure consistent data flows, without manual work, duplicate records or error-prone processes.

## 3. See immediately what is happening and what the impact of changes is
Full insight into your data platform: which data flows exist, how they are connected to each other and what happens when you change something. Logging, monitoring and impact analysis let you find and fix problems quickly.

:::note Lineage
Yres provides visual lineage at the **object level** (tables, views, procedures, functions, materialized views) with impact analysis. Column-level lineage is currently not available.
:::

## 4. Make changes safely without risk to your platform
Yres supports the full lifecycle of your data platform, from development to production. Test changes in advance, roll them out safely and restore complete environments when needed, without data loss.

## 5. Let Azure work for you without managing it yourself
Azure resources are automatically provisioned, scaled and maintained within your own environment. No time spent on complex infrastructure or manual configurations.

## 6. Keep control over access and usage within your organization
Centrally manage who has access to which data. With Azure SSO and role-based permissions (RBAC), everything ties into your existing security and IT environment.
