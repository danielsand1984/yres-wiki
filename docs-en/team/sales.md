## Positioning
- Yres sits **beneath Power BI**, not alongside or in place of it.
- Runs by default in the customer's **own Azure tenant**; hosting by Yres optional.
- Native **Dutch ERP connectors** (Exact, AFAS) as a differentiator.
- Transparent **fixed pricing**. → [Pricing](../prijzen.md)

## Demo
No standard sales demo: a no-obligation conversation with a **data architect**, focused on the current Azure environment. "Within 30 minutes you'll know whether Yres is a fit." → [How it works](../product/hoe-het-werkt.md)

## Competitors (mentioned in interviews/FAQ)
- **TimeXtender**, **AnalyticsCreator** (comparable tooling).
- **Blue Mountain**, **ZIG** (housing corporation sector — experienced by 'thuis as too expensive/too little value).

## Decisive buying criteria (from cases)
Standard connectors · Azure fit · efficiency/cost savings · fewer external hours · central management location · flexibility (no standard reports) · Lean alignment · price-quality · self-management.

## Known area for improvement
Customers find Yres/Plainwater **too modest and insufficiently visible** in the sector — recommendation: more demos, events and trade-journal publications.
