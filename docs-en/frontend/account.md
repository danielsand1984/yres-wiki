# Account

Accessible via the top bar, on the icon with your initials. Here you switch between light/dark mode, open your account settings and send feedback.

## Account settings
Change your **time zone**, **date format** and **language preference**. You can also change your **password**.

> All timestamps are stored in **UTC**; your chosen time zone only affects the display.

## Feedback
Send feedback directly from the frontend. This is emailed to **feedback@yres.app**; replies go to the email address linked to your account.

Feedback is split into three categories:
- **Bug report** — report problems with the application
- **Feature request** — request new or improved features
- **Feedback** — general feedback on Yres or processes
